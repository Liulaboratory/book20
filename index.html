2006 – 2026TWENTY YEARS OF LIGHT

2006 年 12 月，刘小钢老师从 MIT 来到 NUS 化学系，在 Science Drive 2 点亮了一盏灯。那时它只照着一间空空的实验室；二十年后，它照出了 281 篇论文、十二张期刊封面、六十余位校友——其中三十余位已在世界各地建起自己的实验室，把光继续传下去。

左页是二十年的封面、论文与人；右页留白——因为接下来的故事，要由此刻翻开它的人来写。

截至 2026 年，这些工作已被引用逾 77,000 次。但数字只是光的影子；光本身，是每一个在实验台前亮到深夜的人。

谢谢您，刘老师。二十岁生日快乐，刘组。

Liu Lab, Department of Chemistry, National University of Singapore
Liu Lab 20th Anniversary Notebook · 20th Anniversary Edition

In December 2006, Prof. Xiaogang Liu arrived from MIT at the Department of Chemistry, National University of Singapore, and switched on a light at Science Drive 2. At first it shone on an empty laboratory; twenty years on, it has shone through 281 papers, twelve journal covers, and more than sixty alumni — over thirty of whom now lead laboratories of their own around the world, passing the light onward.

On the left-hand pages are twenty years of covers, papers and people; the right-hand pages are left blank — for the story that comes next belongs to whoever opens this book now.

By 2026, this body of work has been cited more than 77,000 times. Yet numbers are only the shadow of light; the light itself is every person who stayed at the bench deep into the night.

Thank you, Prof. Liu — and happy 20th birthday, Liu Lab.

From all the students of Liu Lab · The 20th Anniversary, 2026
